How to use

Camera

Right-drag orbit · Middle-drag or WASD pan · Wheel zoom · F focus selection

Building

Box: click the floor or a box top to place (click a box side to stack against it). Jump Pad / Spawn: click where they go.

Everything snaps to a 0.5 m grid. Hold Alt to place without snapping.

Editing

Click select · Drag move · Shift+click add to selection
Arrows move (Shift = 2 m) · PgUp/PgDn raise/lower
R rotate 90° · Ctrl+D duplicate · Del delete
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo/redo · Esc deselect

Sizes to know

Player is 1.8 m tall · steps up to 0.45 m automatically · jumps about 1.1 m · jump pads throw you about 6.5 m up · slide under anything with 1.2 m+ clearance
